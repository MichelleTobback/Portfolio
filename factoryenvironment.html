Factory Environment - Concrete and Grunge.

I made this scene inside UE5 for the course Environment 1. I started from a blockout using the ue5 modeling tools.

After the blockout, I made several modular assets inside maya to fill out the scene. I also made a trim texture that i baked inside Substance Painter to be used in most of the modular assets.
